# DohaMusicBook — Book Map

## Part 01 — Foundations
Music Intent, listener, genre, mood, reference, 좋은 곡을 판단하는 기준.

## Part 02 — Song Form
Intro, Verse, Pre-Chorus, Chorus, Post-Chorus, Bridge, Outro, section contrast, energy curve.

## Part 03 — Rhythm
Beat, tempo, meter, feel, groove, syncopation, pickup, rhythm patterns.

## Part 04 — Harmony
Key, scale, diatonic harmony, chord function, progression, harmonic rhythm, reharmonization, modulation.

## Part 05 — Melody
Phrase, motif, contour, range, step/leap, tension-resolution, motif development, chord/non-chord tones.

## Part 06 — Lyrics & Vocal
Concept, story, syllable, prosody, rhyme, breath, vocal range, register, expression, harmony/ad-lib.

## Part 07 — Arrangement & Sound
Instrument roles, density, layering, timbre, register, dynamics, stereo image, virtual instruments, samples, SoundFont.

## Part 08 — AI Music
Reference analysis, structured music conditions, candidate generation, variation, regeneration, human review, ranking, similarity.

## Part 09 — MIDI & DAW
MIDI events, velocity, CC, quantization, humanization, plugins, VST, virtual instruments and human editing.

## Part 10 — Audio Production
Recording, sample rate/bit depth, editing, EQ, compression, reverb, delay, saturation, automation, mixing and mastering.

## Part 11 — Rights
Copyright, ownership, neighboring rights, AI contribution, dataset/sample/plugin licenses, commercial use and provenance.

## Part 12 — Demo Project
MusicIntent부터 Final Artifact까지 하나의 곡을 end-to-end로 완성한다.

## Part 13 — DohaStudio Architecture
DohaMusic, DohaLM, DohaAudio, DohaVocal이 음악 제작 흐름에서 담당하는 경계와 연결을 설명한다.

## Part 14 — AI-era Composer
AI에 맡길 것과 사람이 판단할 것, 듣는 귀, 레퍼런스 분석, 포트폴리오와 지속적인 작곡 훈련.
